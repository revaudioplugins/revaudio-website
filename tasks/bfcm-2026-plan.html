Rules

  • "Everything" means every paid plugin on the store. GAS and GAS2 stay free.
  • The bundle is its own Paddle product with its own price. The percentage codes don't stack on it.
  • The bundle is $69 on both Black Friday and Cyber Monday (Gil, Nov 17): one Paddle price, no switch at midnight. 8 plugins, Wide Beam ($9) included.
  • Cyber Monday is a smaller discount than Black Friday. Say it plainly: "Black Friday prices ended Sunday. Cyber Monday is 40% off, today only." Never imply Cyber Monday is the better deal.
  • Real end times on every countdown. No extensions after Dec 1. The honesty rule in PRODUCT.md is the brand.
  • The Halloween BOO code (15%) ends Oct 31 and is removed from the site Nov 1.

Paddle setup (prep)

DRIFT in the bundle

  • DRIFT is sold in the bundle (and on its own at 50% / 40% off), so buyers can buy it from Nov 23.
  • Buyers get the current build right away. When the release build ships a few days later, they get it as a free update with the same licence. If we don't put out a new build, they keep what they have.
  • Copy must say this plainly: "DRIFT is included. You get the current build today, and the release build as a free update when it ships." No release date unless we've fixed one.
  • Before Nov 23: confirm the bundle licence unlocks the DRIFT build that's live, and that the update check or download portal will offer the release build to bundle buyers.
  • The DRIFT beta runs until Fri Nov 20. Everyone who finishes the beta form gets DRIFT free, so they shouldn't be pitched DRIFT itself in the sale emails (the bundle's other plugins, yes).
  • Check: the worker's /drift-feedback handler still mints a $30-off code ($69 → $39) for a finished form (DRIFT_FB_OFF_CENTS). For "free", change it to a 100%-off DRIFT code, or mint the licence directly. Also update the beta page and email copy that still says $39 and Nov 1.

Site themes

Same system as the Halloween skin (commits 9bcfb79 → eb73dff): a seasonal overlay on home + store, page-transition scenes mounted in BaseLayout, and the code next to a prop in the store. Halloween lives on this worktree's branch, so the Black Friday and Cyber Monday skins build on it.

Black Friday skin

"Lights out in the garage"

  • The garage goes black, with one work lamp on the car. Brass and oxide only.
  • The price as a stamped licence plate: "50% OFF" and "BUNDLE $69".
  • Transition scene: the roll-up door slams down to black and comes back up.
  • A real countdown to Sun Nov 29 23:59 PT.
  • A bundle card on the store and home page, listing all 8 plugins and their total value.
Cyber Monday skin

"Diagnostic mode"

  • Same garage, but a scan-tool / ECU screen takes over: green readout, scanlines.
  • "40% OFF" and "BUNDLE $69" on the digital readout.
  • Transition scene: a boot sequence on the dash screen.
  • Countdown to Mon Nov 30 23:59 PT.
  • Same bundle card, new price.

Rules for both skins

  • The skin switches itself by date (PT): Black Friday on Fri Nov 27 00:00, Cyber Monday on Mon Nov 30 00:00, off Tue Dec 1 00:00. The code shown and the bundle price switch from the same date table, so nobody has to push at midnight. Plugin prices on the site stay at list, like Halloween: the code next to the prop is the discount.
  • Early access (Nov 23–26) is email-only: the public site shows normal prices, and the code works at checkout.
  • Reduced-motion versions of every animation. Countdown announced politely (aria-live), visible keyboard focus. WCAG AA like the rest of the site.
  • Test with a date override (e.g. ?now=2026-11-30T08:00-08:00, dev only) to preview each phase before it's live.
  • The push is Dan's call ("push" in chat), same as every live deploy.
