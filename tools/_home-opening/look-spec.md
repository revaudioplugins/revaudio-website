# Long garage: the look spec (v1, 2026-11-04)

The critics' rulebook. Every render is judged against this page, the painting
(`assets/skins/default/paint/walk-000.webp`, the mood) and the reference board. The board's photos stay on Dan's Mac
(`refs/board/`, git-ignored, third-party); `look-board.json` next to this file lists each one's source, author and
licence, so any laptop can fetch them again. Numbers below are board numbers.

## The one line

"I want it to feel like we making plugins" (Dan, 4 Nov). The RevAudio garage is the workshop where the plugins are
built (DESIGN.md §0): a night garage-studio where music gear and car gear live together, and the work in progress is
everywhere.

## Dan, in his words

- The painting: "the vibe itself is amazing" (3 Nov). It sets the mood (light, colour, grime), not the layout.
- "Much more glued and whole, and 3D, and real. Less images." · "it's a garage", not a road · the neon "not in your
  face" · "pristine" · "new with hints of elements from the past, like the GAS 2 vibe, a vintage retro garage /
  music studio", from "real garages and real cool retro messy studios".
- Look lock (4 Nov): Love it on 28 of 32 references; Some of it on 01, 02, 26, 32; none rejected; more references
  like 01 asked for.

## Light: the most important part

- Night. The base is black: whole walls may sit in shadow (10). Light comes from things in the room: amber pendant
  lamps (29, 30), bench and task lamps (08, 10, 12, 13), the neon (09, 20, 21), screens and CRTs (26), lit props (the
  pump globe 16, ON AIR 27), and the street through the door (01, 04).
- The colour pair: violet / magenta neon with amber tungsten practicals (09, 20, 21). Teal and blue only from the
  night outside and from screens (04, 13, 26).
- Pools of light with deep falloff; haze carries it near the door and the lamps (04); bloom only on emitters.
- Chrome and tool handles catch the neon (09). Concrete with a long soft reflection, never a mirror (32).
- The REVAUDIO neon: crooked, warm, not in your face. Any flicker: 3 flashes a second or fewer.

## Materials

- Floor: dark sealed concrete, oil stains, a soft sheen (14, 32).
- Walls: dark, sooty, used (10, 14); wood slat panels and diffusers in the studio corner; pegboards full of tools
  (05, 08, 09).
- Garage metal: red enamel tool chests (05), chrome spanners, steel trusses (31), the ribbed roll-up door (02).
- The past, GAS 2's hints: enamel signs and oil cans (15), a lit pump globe (16), a 1930s-50s service bay (06, 28),
  brushed aluminium and VU meters (24).
- Studio warmth: carpet and rugs (17, 22, 25), wood (25), coloured light pooling on the floor (25).

## Mess and life

- Real garage-band mess (18): cables everywhere, rugs, gear stacked on gear, a CRT, things left mid-use (22, 23).
  Nothing showroom-clean, nothing lined up for a photo.
- Personality objects (the puppet in 23): odd things of our own.
- Someone just got up: the chair pushed back, headphones down, a mug.
- It moves: camera sway, dust in the light, the neon's hum, screens alive, the car idling.

## "We make plugins here"

- The plugins are the point: their CRT screens (must stay), a plugin being built on the bench, sketches and
  schematics pinned up, test gear (a scope with a live trace, 23), boxes and prototypes, a covered COMING SOON object.
- Our names and marks only (RevAudio, RevLimiter, DRIFT, Radio Roulette, RevFX, GAS 2). No third-party brands or
  famous product shapes. Honest: no fake awards, press or testimonials.

## Must stay (checked on every render, wherever each is in view)

RevLimiter's monitor on the desk · Radio Roulette's, high on the right · DRIFT's · RevFX's · the live TV at the end ·
the REVAUDIO neon · the coupe on its lift, half out the door (gloss black, Supra-inspired, no badges) · the drum kit
and throne · the studio corner (panels, diffusers, monitors on stands) · the GAS 2 posters and the COMING SOON plate.

## Differences from the painting (don't copy these)

- Layout: the painting is the 3 Nov room (7.6 x 4.6 m, lens 49); ours is 6.8 x 4.3 x 19.9 m, lens 37.
- The painting dropped the plugin CRTs and garbled the poster text; ours keep both exact.
- The painting's car is a purple coupe; ours is gloss black, Supra-inspired, no badges.
- The painter added brand marks (a Marshall script, headstock decals); ours have none.

## What the critics score (0-10 each, with marked crops)

1. The painting's mood: its light, colour and grime.
2. Light from the room's own things, deep blacks, the colour pair.
3. Mess and life at the level of 18.
4. It reads as "we make plugins here".
5. Every must-stay present and sharp wherever it's in view.
6. No brands, no famous shapes.
7. Glued and whole: nothing looks pasted on.

A render reaches Dan only at 7 or more on every line.
