# Designer B: a working garage, zoned like a real one

**Idea:** a real garage the studio grew into: car bay by the roll-up door, then the bench where car parts and plugins get built, R&D, the band, and the control room at the quiet back end.

**First screen:** on the right, the coupe up on its scissor lift, tail lights glowing, nose out the open door into blue night, a creeper still under it, a trouble light hooked on the tail. On the left, the workbench: RevFX's CRT, the RPM gauge being built under a clamp lamp, spanners on the pegboard. Three amber pendants lead down the aisle to the live TV and the neon. The headline's "CARS." lands over the car. Cars and plugin-building both read in the first second, under one lamp.

**Walk:** 0.2 the bay. 0.4 R&D: Radio Roulette's CRT under a shelf of real car radios, DRIFT's beside the tyre rack, COMING SOON over the build board. 0.5 the band corner and the producer's couch. 0.6 the control room, RevLimiter's CRT on the desk. 1.0 the TV lock.

**Changes and why:**
- Zoned by use, dirty to clean. Bulky storage (parts shelf, hoist, roll cab, tyres, PA, road cases) stands by the people door behind the visitor, keeping sightlines clear.
- Each plugin sits by the car part it came from.
- Lens 46, ceiling 4.0: the door jamb, curtain and lamps frame the car, turned 18° for a rear three-quarter view.
- Neon down, lamps and outside up: warm bench left, cool door right.
- All 10 collisions cleared; CRTs no longer poke through walls; stands no longer block the desk; nothing floats.

**Trade-offs:** 11 props are out of view by the entrance. The sign moved left of the TV to balance the door.
