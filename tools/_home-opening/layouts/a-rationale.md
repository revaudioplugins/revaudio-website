# Designer A: the opening shot

**Idea:** a one-point title shot. The black coupe noses out a night-blue door, a drum kit faces it across the aisle, and every line runs to the REVAUDIO neon and the TV.

**First screen:** three planes, like a poster. Right: the car on its lift, beltline at eye height, tail lights red, its front half silhouetted in the open roll-up door. Left: the GAS 2 pump and FUEL machine, then a drum kit before the amp stack, music answering the car. Far: the crooked neon beside the TV, where both ceiling neons and the amber lamps converge; a shinier floor throws a highlight up the aisle. The centre stays clear for BUILT LIKE CARS. ("CARS." lands on the car). Car, music and plugin screens read in one second.

**The walk:** 0 to 0.2 holds while the headline fades. Near 0.35 the camera slips between kit and car, and the car slides off to reveal a miked engine on a stand. 0.4 to 0.5: the workshop, DRIFT and Radio Roulette CRTs, COMING SOON. 0.5 to 0.6: the studio wall, neon, ON AIR, monitors, RevLimiter on the desk, small kit and throne. 0.8 to 1: the TV fills the screen.

**Changes:** car carZ 0.80, yaw 12, lift 0.7; door 4.0 by 3.5 m, outside 3.0: a whole silhouette, not a cropped box. Neon to the end wall's left at 1.3. Room 4.6 m high so the far lamp clears the neon; lens 40; TV 1.55 m. Aisle cleared, 10 collisions to 0.

**Trade-offs:** the car hides the right wall's low half at walk 0, so a few pieces live out of sight (R12, N09, N10 parked; R15, R17 behind the door). Neon 0.36 and lamps 1.3 keep the leading lines but lift the blacks.
