# Designer C: the walk as the story

**Idea.** Garage to studio: step in under REVAUDIO, meet each car part beside the plugin it became, end at RevLimiter under the live TV.

**First screen.** REVAUDIO hangs crooked over the aisle like a shop sign, right above "BUILT LIKE CARS." The coupe stands whole on its lift, tail lights toward you, half out the open door into blue night. On the left, GAS 2's "Start every mix here." hangs over the kit and pump, then DRIFT's steering-wheel screen and RevFX. Amber pendants lead to the glowing TV. All 14 must-stays are in this frame.

**The reveal.**
- 0.2: DRIFT by its tyres; Radio Roulette and COMING SOON high over the car's roof.
- 0.4: past the car, Radio Roulette on the dash-radio shelf; the studio opens.
- 0.6: kit, throne, monitors on stands, RevLimiter on the desk.
- 0.8 to 1: RevLimiter under the TV, then the lock.

**What changed.**
- Car at carZ 0.80, 14°, lift 0.5, door 3.3 × 3.2 m: a whole hero, not a cropped block.
- Sign moved to the aisle (0.75), as the threshold.
- Plugin bays: DRIFT with tyres, RevFX beside the RPM gauge mid-build, Radio Roulette on its radio shelf.
- Ease 1.6, feel 0.2: every still is a new beat.
- TV 1.7 m. The climax is cleared: ON AIR centred, monitors out to ±1.35 m, chair and mic aside.
- Collisions: 10 to 0.

**Trade-offs.**
- The car hides the low right wall until about walk 0.35, so only high pieces live there.
- Ten duplicate or generic pieces (spare chest and racks, synth, compressor, cabinets, road cases, A-frame, chain hoist) sit behind the start point, out of view.
- The big drum kit shows only in the first frame.
