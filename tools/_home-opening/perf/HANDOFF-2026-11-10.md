## Known findings

- 🔴 **Launch blocker.** revaudio.net's CSP (a Cloudflare transform rule) has no `blob:` in `img-src` and
  `connect-src`, so every textured model renders white (GLTFLoader's embedded images are blob URLs). `script-src` and
  `font-src` also refuse Lenis (jsdelivr) and Google Fonts, which room.html loads: the port must self-host them (use
  the site's own Lenis in `src/lib/motion.ts` and `src/styles/fonts.css`). Dan or Yoni types the two `blob:` tokens into
  the live rule by hand (others edit that rule). Checked 11-10 with curl; the study's stills show it.
- **Gzip makes the loading screen run ahead.** three's FileLoader reports decoded bytes against `Content-Length`,
  which is the compressed size. GitHub Pages gzips `.glb` (checked on a live Pages `.glb`), so `got[src] = 0.95 *
  e.loaded / e.total` (room.html:871) can pass 1 (desk-synth: 194 KB raw, 49 KB gzipped). Fix: totals from the
  known raw sizes, or clamp. The progress also gives no sign of life between the shop light and the end.
- **The study** (`tools/_home-opening/perf/STUDY-2026-11-10.md`, report + the critic's gaps):
  - Options O1-O9.
  - The same-look package O1 + O4 + O3-safe is about frame time, not loading.
  - O9 (right-size textures) saves 2.8 MB; O8 (parallel loading, set up as files land) saves 2-3 s.
  - The critic's gaps: nothing was re-timed on the 82-model room. R2 hosting would ship `.glb` uncompressed (+8.7 MB)
    and needs CORS plus a CSP entry. Windows and a MacBook Air were never measured.
- **Not studied yet, and the big levers for 22 MB:**
  - start the walk once the first view is in and stream the rest along the walk;
  - geometry compression (meshopt or Draco) and KTX2 textures;
  - LODs;
  - a lighter set for slow lines.
- **Dan's options page** (his personal account): https://claude.ai/artifact/G4Mm7W73m2RJeGiQ2hecqF. His picks
  land in ArtifactData collection `picks` (doc = card id): read them first. Its slow-internet card predates this
  measurement (it said "about 2 min, estimate").
